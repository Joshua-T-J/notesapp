A plain notebook that stays on your device.

Nota saves your notes in this browser. No account, no tracking, no ads. Write, tick things off, and export any note as Markdown when you need it somewhere else.

Start writing How your data is handled

Kept on your device

Notes are stored in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, and there are no analytics scripts.

Just writing

A title, your text, and a few tools: headings, bold, lists and checklists. No syntax to learn and no preview pane.

Easy to take with you

Export one note as Markdown or plain text, or download everything at once from Settings.

Sync is on the roadmap

An optional account to sync between devices is planned. Using Nota without an account, with everything kept locally, will stay available.
