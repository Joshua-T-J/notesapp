Privacy policy

Effective {{ site.privacyEffective }} · Applies to the Nota website and app

Summary

Your notes are saved in your own browser and are never sent to us. Nota has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

What is stored, and where

Nota keeps everything in your own browser:

  • your notes, folders and tags, in local storage;
  • images you add to notes, in IndexedDB (the browser’s storage for larger files);
  • your settings, such as theme and whether previews are hidden.

Images are resized and re-saved when you add them, which also removes hidden details such as camera location. This data stays on the device and browser profile where you created it. We cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.

What is not collected

We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. Fonts are served from our own domain, so no font provider sees your visit.

Hosting

The site is hosted by {{ site.host }}. Like any web host, it may record standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to keep the service running and secure. These logs never contain your notes. They are handled under {{ site.host }}’s privacy policy.

Locked notes

Locking a note hides its text on screen until you choose to show it. It does not encrypt the note. Anyone who can use your browser profile could still read it, so do not rely on locking for passwords or other secrets.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export any note as Markdown or plain text, or download all notes from Settings. To delete everything, including images, use Settings › Delete all data, or clear this site’s data in your browser. Clearing browser data removes your notes permanently, so export first if you want to keep them.

Future sync

We plan to offer an optional account for syncing notes between devices. If we do, we will update this policy before it launches, explain what is stored on our servers, and only sync notes for people who sign up. Using Nota without an account will remain possible.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
